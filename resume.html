Equal Tokens, Unequal Losses: Fixed KV-Cache Budgets Penalize High-Fertility Languages

Under review · ACL Rolling Review, October 2026 cycle · Preferred venue: NAACL

  • Investigates whether fixed KV-cache eviction budgets disadvantage high-token-fertility languages in long-context evaluation.
  • Uses NVIDIA kvpress, multilingual Belebele and Qwen2.5, Llama-3.2 and Gemma 3 models for cache eviction and tokenizer-fertility analysis.
  • Explores word-aware eviction as a potential mitigation; no outcome is claimed before analysis.

Citizen Feedback in Digital Government: Trilingual Sentiment Analysis of Bangladeshi Government Play Store Reviews

Submitted to ICCIT 2026 · Under review

  • Cleaned 108,394 collected reviews to 37,241 from 34 government apps across English, Bengali and Banglish; constructed a 501-word and 60-word-pair language lexicon.
  • Benchmarked nine classifiers using weak supervision, with validation against 594 human-labeled reviews and statistical comparisons.
  • BanglaBERT achieved 0.8691 macro-F1; five other models were statistically tied. Measured language disparities and machine-label validation inflation.

BanglaHateMeme: Multimodal Hate Speech and Meme Detection in Bangla Social Media Content

Undergraduate thesis · Journal manuscript in preparation · Supervisor: Dr. Farig Yousuf Sadeque

  • Curated and annotated 5,000+ Bangla social-media memes with protected-category, attack-type and subcategory labels.
